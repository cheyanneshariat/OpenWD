# PG1159 release qualification (2026-09-22)

The refreshed PG1159 adapter uses OpenWD's shared trust-region nonlinear
driver and the development He/C/O NLTE atom. This qualification used the
public PG 1424+535 preset without changing its effective temperature, gravity,
or composition.

## Cold-start result

The standard public calculation started from the package gray atmosphere and
the static, Teff-scaled non-gray temperature initializer. It used 40 depth
points, 120 structural continuum points, two transfer angles, and
`1e-8 <= tau_Ross <= 100`. No atmosphere or population checkpoint was loaded.

The certified structure recorded:

- emergent bolometric flux error: `2.24e-3`;
- maximum local-energy residual over `1e-6 <= tau_Ross < 10`: `1.37e-2`;
- He/C/O population defect: `9.83e-3`;
- final formal-population defect: `9.71e-3`;
- structure time: 522 seconds;
- end-to-end wall time, including trace-element formal line formation: about
  14 minutes on the validation machine.

The certificate deliberately records internal-flux residuals over the
photospheric and complete grids as non-gating diagnostics. The legacy solver
also excluded these ill-conditioned deep cells from its convergence norm and
waived its frozen-population temperature correction after the physical
surface-flux and local-energy checks passed.

## Spectrum and stopping tolerance

The cold-start spectrum was compared with the legacy PG 1424+535 calculation
after normalization at 5500 Angstrom. Over the legacy file's actual
3800--6100 Angstrom coverage, the fractional difference was `6.48e-3` RMS,
`2.30e-3` median, and `9.20e-3` at the 95th percentile. The largest sampled
difference was `5.86e-2` in the 4688 Angstrom line core.

A controlled run tightened only the structural population target from 1% to
0.5%. It increased structure time from 522 to 1046 seconds. Relative to that
control, the 1% spectrum changed by `3.29e-4` RMS, `6.05e-6` median, and
`1.04e-4` at the 95th percentile after continuum normalization. The largest
change was `4.15e-3` at 4688 Angstrom. The public 1% target therefore removes
iteration at the population-map noise floor without a material change to the
optical spectrum.

## Regression checks

The rebuilt optional C extension passed its native interpolation, positive
rate-solver, transfer, and helium-NLTE checks. The complete release test suite
reported 1273 passed, 10 skipped for unavailable optional datasets, and 15
deselected tests. The real cold-start run supplies the end-to-end PG1159
atomic-data coverage skipped by the packaged test environment.

This is one-object numerical qualification. It does not establish a validated
temperature, gravity, or abundance grid, nor does it validate wind physics or
trace-element structural feedback.
