# Dense pure-He DB v6: bound-ion He2+ opacity, dense He- ff, cold tables (2026-10-22)

[History index](README.md) · [Tested points](../../tested-temperature-ranges.md)

The automatic dense-db workflow (`run_model` → `_cool.run_cool_db`, selected
for neutral, non-ideal pure-He photospheres, ~4500-8500 K at log g 8) was
compared with the Montreal pure-He grid (`800_300_ML125_IR`, Blouin et al.
2018 physics below 8000 K) and audited. This note records what changed and
the evidence. Comparisons use band-averaged OpenWD/Montreal flux ratios.

## Physics changes

1. **Bound ions absorb as He2+ (table v6).** The HNC He+ solute uses the
   equal-weight Mayer average of Bruno's bonding and antibonding channels, so
   its excess potential already contains the bound He2+ pairs; the old
   chemistry also added an explicit He2+ species (double count, <=20% in n_e
   at 0.01-0.1 g/cm3), and its opacity gave every ion He.He+ free-free.
   `bound_neighbour_count` integrates the bonding-channel pairs with relative
   energy below zero, N_b = n ∫ ½ exp(-V/kT + γ) P(3/2, -V/kT) d³r, from the
   same HNC solution. As n -> 0 it equals n/K(He2+) of the Chang bound levels
   to 3-5% (5000-10000 K). `solve_solute_chemistry` ionizes through the single
   solute reaction and splits ions into bound He2+ (fraction 1 - exp(-N_b);
   Stancil photodissociation) and free He+ (He.He+ free-free). At photospheric
   densities f_bound >= 0.97, the opacity mix of Saumon et al. (2022) Fig. 18
   (He- ff + He2+ bound-free + CIA + Rayleigh, no He.He+ free-free).
2. **Dense-fluid He- free-free** (Rogers-Young S(k), no 1/|ε|²; see
   [the DQ note](dq-dense-he-ff-2026-10-15.md)) is applied by the dense-db
   opacity, run and audit (`--dense-heminus-ff`, default on; checksum in the
   physics label). The table is extended from 2412 to 861 K with
   `tools/extend_dense_heminus_table.py`; cold isotherms keep only densities
   where the fluid solve converges and requests above are refused.
3. **He2+ photodissociation below 4200 K.** Stancil's table starts at 4200 K;
   the held values overestimated the optical cross section by up to ~15-50x at
   1000-4200 K and put a kink in a dominant opacity. `tools/build_he2_photodissociation.py`
   computes σ(λ, T) (Chang X levels, Bruno A curve + polarization, dipole R/2;
   1.1-1.4x Stancil with the same shape at 4200-12600 K); only its temperature
   dependence is applied to Stancil's 4200 K values.

## Domain and numerics

- Electron-insertion fit (Kowalski thesis Eqs. 164-166): density domain 2 ->
  4 g/cm3 (its DFT data span 0.5-4 g/cm3, Fig. 31); temperature floor 0.1 ->
  0.001 eV (the thesis shows it to 10 K against 10-100 K data, Fig. 32).
- Interaction table: 165 densities to 4.07 g/cm3 and 91 temperatures from
  830 K. The builder is single-process, checkpointed, retries each state from
  the previous density, the adjacent isotherm and a cold start, then density
  substeps (the deep-well He+ Krylov solve otherwise stalls or overflows).
  `maximum_valid_density` records where cold isotherms stop; the EOS refuses
  requests above it (`DenseHeliumDomainError`).
- `_mass_feautrier`: where the accumulated optical depth has lost the
  increments of nearly transparent layers (cm wavelengths of the 1e9 Å audit
  grid), face resistances use the trapezoidal κΔm directly; other cases are
  unchanged bit for bit. The audit skips its non-qualifying optical-depth
  diagnostic, with the reason recorded, when τ is not strictly increasing.

## Results (log g 8, fresh `run_cool_db` cold starts, default settings)

All seven v6 models converge and pass the independent audit (10-14 min each);
main (e75a113) fails at 4500 K (flux residual 0.12, F = 0.916 σT⁴).

| Teff | rms ln(OpenWD/Montreal), main | v6 | v6 1500-2500 Å | v6 1.2-2.5 µm |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 4500 | not converged | 0.759 | 6.62 | 0.65 |
| 5000 | 0.555 | 0.526 | 3.80 | 0.69 |
| 5500 | 0.332 | 0.306 | 2.18 | 0.77 |
| 6000 | 0.182 | 0.153 | 1.48 | 0.86 |

From 6500 K the models agree with Montreal to ~1-3% over 3000 Å-1.2 µm.

## Considered and not adopted

- **Free-state-only He+ ("v5").** Removing the bound part of the bonding
  channel from the He+ solute removes the double count but also the
  many-neighbour hole stabilization: the effective ionization energy at
  0.7-1 g/cm3 becomes 24-25 eV, versus ~20 eV for the GW band gap of
  Kowalski's thesis (v4/v6: 19.0-21.1 eV). Photospheres then run past the
  table at 5000 K.
- **Kowalski (2007) ionization strength**, as in the Montreal models (log x_e
  ≈ -7.6 at 1 g/cm3, 0.5 eV; effective ionization ~15.7 eV), contradicts the
  GW gap and the empirical ~20.5 eV of Farihi et al. (2026). As a labelled
  diagnostic at 5000 K it closes most of the near-IR deficit (rms 0.526 ->
  0.486); with Blouin's PY + 1/|ε|² He- ff as well, rms 0.461, but the UV
  excess stays 3.4x.

## Open

Below ~5500 K the models remain bluer than Montreal (UV 2-6x, near-IR
0.65-0.77). Montreal's documented ionization and He- ff do not account for the
UV; the cause is not identified. He.He+ free-free is still held at 4200 K
(minor; nearly all cool ions are bound). Refraction is absent.
