# Grid coverage

The final grids contain 1,542 hydrogen-and-helium spectra and 1,523 spectra with metals.

[![Temperature and gravity coverage](coverage-temperature-gravity.png)](coverage-temperature-gravity.pdf)

Each cell counts the spectra available at 11 helium abundances, from log10(He/H) = −5 to 0. The horizontal axis is temperature in kelvin. The vertical axis is log10 of surface gravity in cm s⁻². Dark blue means all 11 spectra are available. Lighter cells have missing spectra.

The metal spectra include hydrogen and helium, with C/N/O/Si/S in NLTE and Ca in LTE at one fixed composition. The two panels show separate grids; their counts are not added together. Failed points remain masked. Physical accuracy and interpolation accuracy are not established.
