Each cell counts qualified spectra across the 11 surface gravities log g = 4.8–6.8 (cgs) at one temperature and helium abundance. The top panels show the selected grid; the lower panels show additions from the longer runs. A zero means no qualified spectrum at those coordinates. It is not a zero flux or an interpolated model. H/He and metals use different numerical checks. Metals use one fixed composition on each accepted H/He structure. No independent abundance axes or physical/interpolation accuracy are established.
