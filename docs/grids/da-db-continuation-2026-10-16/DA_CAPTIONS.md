DA source comparisons. The left panels show the accepted predecessors (black dashed) and fresh c103b53 spectra (blue solid). The right panels show 100(F_new-F_old)/F_old on the identical native vacuum wavelength points. The surface flux density F_lambda is in physical units; dividing the displayed flux by 10^8 changes only the axis units. No flux normalization, wavelength resampling or extrapolation was applied. The UV and optical ranges are 1150–3000 and 3000–10000 Angstrom, respectively.

The rows show the largest integrated absolute UV change in each of three temperature groups among all 804 matched accepted DA pairs: Teff <= 5000 K (106 pairs), 5000 < Teff <= 20000 K (409 pairs), and Teff > 20000 K (289 pairs). The selected coordinates are 3750 K/log g 6.0, 10500 K/log g 6.0, and 24000 K/log g 8.25. The same coordinates appear in both figures. These are deliberately selected examples, not typical spectra. Difference scales vary by row. The cool model has very little UV flux, and pointwise relative differences in that band must be interpreted with the absolute flux panel.

All 852 original DA coordinates were attempted once. The fresh source passed the declared native numerical checks for 838; 34 of those have no accepted predecessor and are excluded from pair statistics. Four accepted older models are retained after fresh failure. The 14 fresh failures remain separate and do not enter the plotted differences. The full-source comparison does not isolate an individual fix or establish physical accuracy, independent depth convergence, interpolation accuracy or agreement with observations. The frozen c103b53 source is older than upstream main 00503a0. Public mapped adoption has not yet been performed.
